**Jun 15, 2026 — Mark:Jayesh:Romeo Standup**

### Summary
Meeting focused on resolving Google Fi promotional display challenges and finalizing essential website copy and logic.

**Promotional messaging and data**
Messaging for internet savings will use ranges rather than exact figures. The team decided to treat 50GB plans as unlimited for the duration of the current promotional period.

**Technical implementation strategy**
A pro tip UI element will be implemented for the minimum viable product launch to highlight Google Fi promotions. Government fees will be applied to the original pricing.

**Infrastructure and roadmap**
Infrastructure issues regarding Amazon Web Services were resolved. Future strategy includes an August launch for bundle savings features aligned with back to school promotions.

### Decisions

**Aligned**
- **Hassle-free internet messaging updated** — The copy for 'hassle-free internet' will utilize 'up to' or range-based savings language rather than stating fixed, specific savings amounts.
- **Google Fi plan categorization adjusted** — Google Fi data plans (50GB and 100GB) will be treated as 'unlimited' within the search engine for the duration of the promotion to ensure they surface in search results.
- **Promotional backend feature added to roadmap** — The engineering team will add a feature request to the product roadmap to enable backend database flagging for future promotions, eliminating the need for manual database updates.
- **Google Fi promotion UI strategy selected** — The Google Fi promotion will be displayed in the UI as a 'pro tip' element showing expiration dates and incremental savings, rather than a standard search engine result card.
- **Government fee calculation rule set** — Government fee calculations will be applied to the undiscounted, original price of the plan rather than the discounted, promotional price.
- **Google Fi promotion scope defined** — The Google Fi promotion is scoped strictly for new accounts only and will not be surfaced or offered to existing customers.
- **Primary promotional plan selected** — The 30GB Google Fi plan is selected as the primary promotional plan to be surfaced as the absolute cheapest option.

### Next steps
- **[Jayesh Gohil] Update Copy:** Modify the hassle free internet website text to display a savings range up to 40 dollars.
- **[Jayesh Gohil] Implement Promotion:** Configure the search engine to display Google Fi offers as a pro tip until the end of the month.
- **[Jayesh Gohil] Fix Fee Calculation:** Apply 7 percent government fees to the undiscounted price for new customer accounts.
- **[Jayesh Gohil] Update Site Images:** Refine website imagery using the provided source picture and LinkedIn photo.
- **[Jayesh Gohil] Verify Price Data:** Check the recently refreshed internet pricing data for accuracy.

Romeo Reyes also noted that internet pricing data had been refreshed the previous week and is available for reference (00:33:48).
- **Long-Term Roadmap and Bundle Strategy:** The team discussed future initiatives, including the potential for a "bundle savings" column to display effective internet pricing when combined with wireless plans.
