### 1. 🎯 PRICING MODEL FLIP — $7.99/mo → $49.99/year

- Includes broader Reyly membership benefits + unlimited switching for the year
- Multi-line pricing floated: $49.99 for first 3 lines, ~$10/line after (TBD)

### 2. Port-Out PIN — Carrier Dial Codes 🎯
- **Fastest method locked in as primary UX path:**
  - AT&T: `*7678` → prompts for account passcode → texts port-out PIN
  - Verizon: `#7678`
  - T-Mobile: `#7678#`
- `7678` = spells **PORT** on keypad
- **Prioritize dial codes over app or desktop portals** in Switch Kit guide

### 3. "Step Zero" — Account Passcode Prerequisite 🎯
- **Critical distinction surfaced:** account passcode ≠ port-out PIN
- Account passcode is REQUIRED to generate port-out PIN via dial codes
- ~99% of users don't know their account passcode (echoes Jul 6 insight)
- **Decision:** add Step Zero to DIY guide → "make sure you know or reset your account passcode BEFORE Step 1"
- Mark + Alan to send Jayesh reset instructions for Verizon + T-Mobile
