**Problem:** Romeo emailed "why did the engine suggest X instead of Visible/Mint for a $220/3-line Verizon user?" — questions eating Jayesh's time.

**Solution (new admin panel tool):**
- Sidebar: **Plans & Providers → Saving Engine Lab**
- Team enters: provider + line count + monthly amount
- Runs the engine live → **shows the reasoning** for every suggestion
- **Example walkthrough** (Romeo's actual query):
  - Q: Why not suggest Visible for $220 Verizon user?
    - **A:** Visible = **postpaid**; max-savings engine is set to **prepaid-only**
  - Q: Why suggest Total Wireless over Mint (Mint is cheaper)?
    - **A:** DB has fees on Mint but **zero fees on Total Wireless** → post-fee calc favors Total Wireless
- Alan: "It's a lot like AI now — you can say 'show your work'"
- **Effect:** stops team from pestering Jayesh with logic questions — self-service
