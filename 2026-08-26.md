- Mark: NOT a general feedback widget — scoped to switch flow screens only
- Purpose: friction relief for users who don't want to complete guided steps
- Preserves Flex Offers commission on bypass

### 6. 🎨 3/6/12 Month Prepaid UI — NEEDS REDESIGN (confusing)
- **Problem:** current design shows 3/6/12-month prices flat — users think "$720" is monthly cost
- Romeo: "This looks a little confusing... doesn't say that that's the savings"
- **Proposed structure:** tabs / higher-level selection layer for prepay duration
  - Pay upfront for 3 months → **Total Wireless wins** (Mint's 3-month has fees)
  - Pay upfront for 6 months → shifts to Mint
  - Pay upfront for 12 months → Mint wins (best deal in marketplace)
- Recommended provider **dynamically changes** based on duration selection
- Mark + Jayesh agreed
