## Topics Discussed

### 1. Free Trial and Perks Policy
- Perks are visible, claimable, and redeemable during the free trial period
- Users are permitted to upgrade to a paid membership before the 7-day free trial ends

### 2. Affiliate Membership Deactivation Policy
- If an affiliate is deactivated, their free membership stays active until end of current billing cycle (typically end of month)
- Exception: immediate termination only if deactivation is due to egregious abuse of the system
- Deactivation reasons discussed: lack of productivity or business disagreement — membership still maintained until billing period ends

### 3. Affiliate Commission Structure
- Model: Revenue share, staggered over 90 days to incentivize customer retention
- Payment schedule:
  - Day 30 → **$5**
  - Day 60 → **$5**
  - Day 90 → **$10**
- Condition: affiliate only gets paid if the customer remains an active member at each interval
- Only commissions from active memberships are shown in the affiliate's dashboard
- Any fully earned amount must be fully accessible

### 4. Affiliate Payout Minimum Threshold
- Minimum wallet balance of **$100** required before affiliates can withdraw
- Reason: reduces payment gateway fees on small transfers and encourages affiliates to bring in more customers

### 5. Scope of Affiliate Commissions
- Commissions apply **only to membership acquisition cost** — not on other product sales (digital passes, Flex Offers, etc.)
- Rationale: low profit margins on items like digital passes make per-sale commission unviable
- Lump-sum customer acquisition model preferred for initial phase

### 6. Duration of Affiliate Commissions
- Commissions paid for **first 3 months only** of a customer's subscription
- Avoids a perpetual annuity model per referred user

### 7. Flex Offers Integration Status
- Mark confirmed contact has been made with Flex Offers
- Flex Offers is re-reviewing the application with their compliance group
- Previous issue: incomplete business description in original application
- Team hopes to hear back soon to use Flex Offers in staging environment

### 8. Payment Gateway — PayPal & Apple Pay via Stripe
- Jayesh sent activation links to Mark for PayPal and Apple Pay within Stripe
- Activating via Stripe eliminates the need for a direct PayPal integration
- Mark to activate both payment options

### 9. Affiliate Onboarding Process
- Currently manual: affiliates created via admin panel only — no frontend self-service sign-up yet
- Flow: admin sets up affiliate → affiliate receives email → affiliate accesses dedicated panel with referral link and earnings view
- Jayesh to develop and add a frontend affiliate sign-up feature
