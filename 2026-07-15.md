- More granular: shows exact plans available at a specific home

- **Current:** logic restricted to **fixed wireless only**

- **Decision:** REMOVE fixed-wireless constraint → recommend **cheapest available plan regardless of technology** (fiber/cable/fixed wireless/satellite) based on user address
- Matches mobile logic: pick lowest price, ignore technology

### 5. Bundle Discount Logic — DB Schema Update 🎯
- Two new DB columns:
  - **`required_provider`** = Yes/No flag (e.g., Visible internet only for Visible wireless users)
  - **`provider_for_bundle_discount`** = **comma-separated provider names** (for multi-provider bundle scenarios)
- **Bundle eligibility clarification (Romeo):**
  - **Same-company only:** Visible+Verizon, Mint+Mint, T-Mob+T-Mob
