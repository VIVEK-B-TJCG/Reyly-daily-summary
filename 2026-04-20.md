# 2026-04-20 — Reyly Team Meeting

## Attendees
- Mark Gilmor
- Romeo Reyes
- Martin Lerda
- Jayesh Gohel

## Topics Discussed

### 1. Flex Offers Query — Initial Assessment
- Jayesh raised a query with Flex Offers re: seller names and products
- Received ticket email — no further response over the weekend
- Investigation found: **Visible only offers affiliate links for mobile devices (iPhones, watches)** — NO service plan links
- Current Flex Offers setup only allows redirecting users to the general Visible website, not plan-specific pages

### 2. Flex Offers Limitation — Hardware-Only Links
- Confirmed by Mark and Jayesh: Flex Offers does NOT provide affiliate links for specific service plans, only for hardware purchases
- Visible payout: **$58 per activation** — but links are strictly for devices
- Core concern: if Visible only pays commission on device purchases (not plans), tracking service conversions becomes unproductive

### 3. Variation Across Providers
- Problem with Visible doesn't apply uniformly — some providers DO offer both devices and plans
- **Straight Talk:** 103 products listed, including data plans (both devices and plans available)
- **Mint Mobile:** offers sales plans in 3, 6, and 12-month chunks

### 4. Mint Mobile Availability & Economics
- Jayesh applied for Mint — currently under review
- Mint Mobile payout: **$160 per online sale or call conversion** (high)
- Comparison: Straight Talk pays only **$9.60**
- Very large variation in payouts across providers

### 5. Provider Availability on Flex Offers
- Only **21 wireless providers** available on Flex Offers
- Some programs (e.g., **Boost Mobile**) are restricted and require special approval
- **Major internet providers like Xfinity are NOT listed** on Flex Offers
- Available wireless providers include: Boost, Mint, Straight Talk (among others)

### 6. Alternative Affiliate Networks — Impact & CJ
- Flex Offers easy to join but limited — team considering Impact and CJ (Commission Junction) as alternatives
- **Impact requires minimum 3,000 monthly visitors** (traffic constraint)
- Digital passes and coupons complicate the model — customers purchase them from the provider's website, not Reyly's platform

### 7. Strategy: Deep Linking + Data Scraping
- For providers like Visible (no plan-specific links) → team can:
  - **Scrape plan data and feed it into Reyly's database**
  - Redirect users to the correct plan page directly
  - Accept no affiliate commission initially (until the provider offers it)
- Program availability changes daily/monthly — requires a system to curate which plans are currently active

### 8. Direct Outreach to Impact and CJ
- Mark suggested reaching out to Impact and CJ directly
- Goal: explain the platform, potentially waive traffic requirement
- Argument: investment and seriousness may hold weight
- Martin assigned to initiate these direct conversations

## Decisions Made

| Decision | Reason | Owner |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Continue with Flex Offers as primary while pursuing Impact/CJ in parallel | Flex is easiest entry point; Impact/CJ block on 3K monthly traffic | Team |
| For providers without plan-specific affiliate links → scrape data and deep-link to correct page | Better UX; still redirects user accurately even without commission | Team |
| Start with Flex Offers, pursue Impact later after increasing traffic | Realistic staging given traffic requirement | Team |
| Contact Impact and CJ directly to explain platform & negotiate traffic requirement | Demonstrating seriousness may waive the 3K minimum | Mark + Martin |
| Build curation system for active/inactive affiliate programs | Program availability changes daily/monthly | Jayesh |
